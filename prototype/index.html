Vacancy & Hiring Tracker · design preview

Every approved position, its stage, its documents and who is blocking it.

Five screens of the internal tracker, wired together on one shared dataset. Switch the signed-in identity in the top right of any page and the navigation, queues, table scope and candidate document access all change — that is the permission model, shown rather than described.

01 Dashboard Role-aware action queue, position ledger, eight count tiles where every tile opens a drill-down list of exactly that count, stage pipeline, ageing and overdue, facility breakdown, live activity timeline. Open → 02 Vacancy requests Scoped register plus the raise-request drawer, with a live preview of the position records the quantity will create. Open → 04 My Actions Personal approval queue with SLA rails. Decide on a vacancy or a candidate and the item leaves the queue. Open → 03 Request detail Positions, candidates, resume and photograph requirements, hold overlay, four ageing clocks, timeline, comments. Open → 05 Admin Panel Users, role × permission matrix, workflow configuration, facilities, versioned legal documents, retention and the append-only audit log. Open →
Synthetic demo data only. No real candidate names, resumes, photographs or contact details appear anywhere on this site, and nothing is stored — there is no backend behind this preview. Not for external sharing.
